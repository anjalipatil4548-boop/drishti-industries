We map fields on a fixed schedule: the same lines, at the same height, every week. The CF-1211 Survey covers 45 ha per sortie at 1.3 cm/px and reads crop vigour, soil moisture and standing water in five bands, so this week's map sits beside last week's without correction.

Scheme.

Markers 01–04 follow the method below.

Field — 45 ha / sortie5-band swathLaunchReturnLines at 100 m01020304

Method.

  1. 01

    Set the field.

    You draw the field boundary. The float plans parallel lines at 100 m, with 80% forward and 70% side overlap.

  2. 02

    Fly the lines.

    The CF-1211 flies each line at 12 m/s and turns outside the boundary: 45 ha per sortie, in winds up to 15 m/s.

  3. 03

    Read the bands.

    A 5-band multispectral swath at 5.3 cm/px returns crop vigour, soil moisture and standing water, plot by plot.

  4. 04

    Fly it again.

    Next week the float flies the same lines at the same hour, so each plot is read against its last map.
