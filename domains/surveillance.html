Surveillance.

CoverPersist

  • Cover

  • Persist

We keep wide areas and long lines in view from the air: rail, roads, pipelines and the ground beside them. Floats fly a loop with overlapping fields of view, so no stretch goes unseen between passes. They observe places and assets; people are detected only so they can be blurred.

Scheme.

Markers 01–04 follow the method below.

CorridorField of viewOverlapPatrol loop — 2 h 30 minHold01020304

Method.

  1. 01

    Draw the corridor.

    You draw the line and how far either side to record. The float plans a loop along it, below 120 m.

  2. 02

    Overlap the views.

    Fields of view overlap from one station to the next, so every stretch of the corridor is seen twice per pass.

  3. 03

    Fly the loop.

    The ZZ-R12 keeps the loop going for 2 h 30 min; a CF-1212 holds the stretch that needs a fixed view.

  4. 04

    Flag in flight.

    Detection runs on board at 30 fps. Fallen trees, open gates and heat faults are flagged while the float is in the air.
