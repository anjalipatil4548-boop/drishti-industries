We send a float before anyone travels. The ZZ-R12 Long flies out along a line, up to 55 km from launch, and records the ground in 4K and thermal. It turns when the energy it needs to come home falls to a 20% reserve, a figure it checks every 30 s, wherever it is on the line.

Scheme.

Markers 01–04 follow the method below.

Method.

  1. 01

    Draw the line.

    You draw one line over the ground. The float turns it into waypoints 90 m above the terrain, below the 120 m ceiling.

  2. 02

    Fly out.

    It flies the line at cruise, recording 4K video from a 30× zoom camera and radiometric thermal.

  3. 03

    Check the energy.

    Every 30 s it works out the energy to fly home in the wind it has measured, and adds a 20% reserve.

  4. 04

    Turn and return.

    When the energy on board meets that figure, it turns, and reports any part of the line it has not reached.
